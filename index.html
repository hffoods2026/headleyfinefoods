Our story

Family-run. Community-minded. Full of flavour.

Headley Fine Foods is a family-run business that holds dear the importance of a traditional café and the sense of community that comes with it, while putting a modern twist on the food we serve.

We pride ourselves on bringing together famous dishes and flavours from across the world, giving our customers the chance to enjoy something a little different without leaving Headley. From vibrant bowls and flatbreads to homemade bakes and café classics, our counter is always evolving.

Wherever possible, we combine those global influences with quality local produce — bringing flavours from across the continents to our village while keeping quality, freshness and community at the centre of what we do.
